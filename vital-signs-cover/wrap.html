RELIGION / CHRISTIAN MINISTRY / PASTORAL RESOURCES

Every church takes on the pulse of the one who leads it.

When a church is struggling, we usually treat the church. Attendance dips, so we change the service. Volunteers wear out, so we recruit harder. The symptoms ease for a season and then come right back, because the trouble started somewhere else.

Vital Signs starts with the leader. Mark Merrill traces how a pastor is formed and how that formation, healthy or not, shows up in the life of a congregation. Then he lays out a way back to health that begins in the leader’s own soul and moves outward into the church.

For pastors who are tired of treating symptoms, and for the elders and boards who want to help them get well.
